Manchester United boss Carrick addresses Bruno Fernandes captaincy row

Manchester United manager Michael Carrick has accepted responsibility for the club's poor run of form following a 4-1 Champions League defeat to Como.

Speaking at a press conference, Carrick acknowledged supporters' frustrations and insisted he remains confident the team can rediscover its identity.

Carrick also denied that the dressing room was divided after captain Bruno Fernandes appeared to question the team's tactics.
